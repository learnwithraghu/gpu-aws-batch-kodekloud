# Video 03 — Mutable latest
**Type:** Theory  
**Runtime target:** ~3–4 minutes

---

**What `:latest` means.** “Whatever was pushed most recently to this tag.” **Convenient** for courses; **risky** for reproducible prod.

**Why teams pin.** Incident replay: “run **digest sha256:…** again.” Regulated industries keep immutable tags.

**Teaching choice.** Rebuild, push, submit — no job-def change when URI ends in **`latest`**.

**Visual:** Fast lane (latest) vs audit lane (digest pin).
